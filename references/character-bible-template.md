## Character quality test

A recurring character is ready only when:

- its silhouette remains identifiable in solid black;
- it has at least three identifiers beyond color;
- another character cannot inherit its accessory without confusion;
- its teaching function is clear;
- the description avoids relying on a name, franchise, logo, or outside reference;
- the invariant block fits into 60–120 words for repeated image prompts.

## Reference sheet

Create one portrait sheet containing front, three-quarter, side, three expressions, one action pose, and a scale comparison. Keep text minimal. Approve it before generating pages and save it under the project’s `character-references/` folder.
